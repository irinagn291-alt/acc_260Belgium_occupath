Occupath is a Marey desk for compiling a working timetable. Time runs down, distance runs across. The Marey is the occupation, not a train list. It is for heritage-railway volunteers, model railroaders, and amateur working-timetable compilers.

• Paint the path. Place a path opens Path. Enter Consist, Length, and passing times at mileposts, then tap Paint the path.
• Enter measurement. Measurement records From milepost, To milepost, Tape, Clino, and Bearing.
• Close the loop. Tape, clino and bearing reduce to a traverse. The desk shows a Depth section and a loop report with Loop feasible or Loop in exception. The board clears only when the loop holds the shorter consist.
• Hand the token. Token occupation: one token per single-line block. Take the token or Hand the token. A second consist is refused until you hand it.
• Accept meet. Meet rewrites the later path. Inspector lists Conflicts, Token hops, and Offer a meet.
• Commit the week. Commit the board commits the working board and opens the next ISO week. Week ledger lists working boards by ISO week.
• Settings holds Contact Occupath, Re-run onboarding, and Reset all data.

Working boards stay on this device. There is no account and no in-app purchase.
